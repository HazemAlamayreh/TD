# ATD Academy — Mastery Check Answer Key — Domain 2


## 2.1 Learning Sciences

- **2.1.1-M1 — C.** Constructivist approaches emphasize authentic experiences and learner-generated meaning.

- **2.1.1-M2 — B.** Authentic problem solving supports construction of knowledge; repetition of recall alone does not develop adaptive reasoning.

- **2.1.2-M1 — D.** Segmenting and removing extraneous material helps preserve limited working-memory resources.

- **2.1.2-M2 — A.** Segmenting and removing unnecessary duplication reduces extraneous processing; accessible transcripts can remain available on demand.

- **2.1.3-M1 — A.** Knowles’s adult-learning principles emphasize relevance, experience and a problem-centered orientation.

- **2.1.3-M2 — C.** Adults benefit from relevant problems that draw on experience and involve immediate application; learner choice alone is insufficient.

- **2.1.4-M1 — B.** Coordinated visuals and narration limit avoidable cognitive load while keeping information accessible.

- **2.1.4-M2 — D.** Clear aligned narration and visuals reduce redundancy while retaining access; repetition of the same overloaded material does not solve it.

- **2.1.5-M1 — C.** Repeated retrieval distributed over time supports retention and application.

- **2.1.5-M2 — B.** Spaced recall plus later applied observation addresses retention and actual transfer, unlike an immediate repeated test.
